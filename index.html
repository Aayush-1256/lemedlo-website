How it works

Medicine reminder setup
in under 3 minutes

No complex setup. Just add your family, add their medicines, and let LeMedLo handle the rest.

1
Create your family
Start a family group in seconds. Share the invite code with family members — they join instantly. Works for parents, grandparents, anyone.
2
Add each person's medicines
Add medicine names, doses, and reminder times for each member. Set how many pills are left — we'll warn you before they run out.
3
Get smart reminders
Everyone gets notified at exactly the right time. Tap "Taken" or "Skip" — we track it all so you never lose sight of who took what.
4
Track & celebrate progress
Watch adherence climb month after month. Get weekly summaries, missed dose alerts, and low stock warnings. Your family's health, visualised.

Best Free Medicine Reminder App India – LeMedLo

LeMedLo is the best free medicine reminder app for India. Whether you need a dawaa reminder, tablet reminder, pill reminder, or full medication tracker, LeMedLo covers it all. Available on Android and iOS, LeMedLo is designed specifically for Indian families who manage medicines for parents, grandparents, and children at home.

Dawaa Reminder & Tablet Reminder for Every Dose

Never forget a dawaa again with LeMedLo's smart dawaa reminder system. Set tablet reminders for morning, afternoon, and night. Get push notifications for every scheduled dose. If a dose is missed, LeMedLo sends a follow-up alert so you can still mark it taken or skip it. The app tracks every taken, missed, and skipped dose automatically.

Medicine Reminder App for Elderly Parents

Managing medicines for Mummy, Papa, Dadi, or Nana has never been easier. LeMedLo is the ideal medicine reminder app for elderly patients in India. The family head (usually an adult child) adds all medicines and reminder times on behalf of elders. The elderly person just needs to see the notification — no app navigation required.

BP Medicine Reminder and Diabetes Medicine Reminder

LeMedLo is especially helpful for chronic condition medicines like BP tablet reminders, diabetes medicine reminders (sugar ki dawaa), thyroid medicine reminders, heart medicine reminders, and cholesterol medicine reminders. Set a recurring daily reminder once and LeMedLo handles it every day — with full adherence history visible on the dashboard.

Family Medicine Management App – Manage the Whole Family

LeMedLo is a complete family medicine management app. Add your entire family — spouse, kids, parents, in-laws — in one app. Track each person's medicines separately. The family head dashboard shows the total medicines taken, pending, and missed across all family members. LeMedLo is the only family medication tracker built for Indian joint families.

Medication Adherence Tracking – Monthly Reports

Poor medication adherence is one of the leading causes of health problems in India. LeMedLo's medication adherence tracking shows you exactly how consistent each family member has been with their medicines. View 30-day adherence percentages, monthly trend graphs, and dose-by-dose history — all inside the app for free.

Free Medicine Tracker App – No Subscription

LeMedLo is a completely free medicine tracker app. There are no subscription fees, no hidden charges, and no in-app purchases needed to use core features. Download the free pill reminder and medicine tracker on Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone/iOS) and start tracking your family's medicines instantly.
