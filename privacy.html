Privacy Policy

How LeMedLo collects, uses and protects your health data

LeMedLo is built for Indian families. We collect only what is necessary to deliver medicine reminders and family health tracking. We never sell your data.

1 Who we are

LeMedLo ("we", "us", "our") is a health reminder application operated by LeMedLo. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@lemedlo.app.

2 What data we collect

Account information: Your mobile number (used for OTP login). Your display name and avatar initials you choose.

Family data: Names, ages, relationships and phone numbers of family members you add. This data is entered by you voluntarily.

Medicine data: Medicine names, dosages, schedules and stock levels you add for yourself and your family members.

Health logs: Blood pressure readings, blood sugar readings and notes you log inside the app.

Doctor appointments: Doctor name, specialty, clinic, appointment date and any notes you add.

Dose logs: Records of when doses were marked taken, skipped or missed, and by whom.

Device data: Your device's push notification token (to deliver reminders). Your device platform (Android or iOS).

Chat messages: In-app chat messages between family members about medicines, stored to show conversation history.

3 How we use your data

  • To send you local dose reminder notifications at the times you set
  • To allow your family head to monitor your medicine adherence
  • To alert family heads when a dependent misses or skips a dose
  • To send cross-device push notifications (e.g. FH reminder to dependent)
  • To maintain your health log history (BP, blood sugar)
  • To remind you about upcoming doctor appointments
  • To keep the in-app chat history between family members

We do not use your data for advertising profiling, sell it to third parties, or use it for any purpose beyond operating the LeMedLo service.

4 Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate LeMedLo:

  • Supabase — Database and authentication. Your data is stored on Supabase servers (AWS ap-northeast-2, Seoul region). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Expo / Expo Push Notifications — Delivery of cross-device push notifications. Expo Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — We show banner and interstitial ads in the free version of the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalisation subject to your device's ad tracking settings. Google Privacy Policy

5 Data storage and security

Your data is stored in a secured Supabase database protected by Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Each user can only access data belonging to their own families. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

We do not store your data on our own servers. All data resides with Supabase on AWS infrastructure.

6 Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, contact us at support@lemedlo.app and we will process your deletion request within 30 days.

Dose logs and health logs are kept indefinitely so you have a full history. You can delete individual health log entries inside the app at any time.

7 Children's privacy

LeMedLo allows family heads to add dependent family members including children. Information about children (name, age, medicines) is entered by an adult family head and is used solely to manage that child's medicine reminders. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13.

8 Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (directly in the app or by contacting us)
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent for push notifications at any time via your device settings
  • Opt out of personalised ads via your device's advertising settings (Limit Ad Tracking on iOS / Opt out of Ads Personalisation on Android)

To exercise any right, email us at support@lemedlo.app.

9 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date below. Continued use of LeMedLo after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10 Contact us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

Email: support@lemedlo.app
